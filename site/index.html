01 / THE VISION

Knowledge should connect, not disappear into silos.

Useful discoveries happen everywhere—in local reporting, scientific papers, community discussions, and independent investigations. Voalt is designed to help those discoveries find one another, with sources, context, and corrections kept close at hand.
