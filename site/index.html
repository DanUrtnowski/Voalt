About

What Voalt is

What it is. Voalt is a software brand. Design, development, testing, and support are done by Dan.

What is built. Applied software in three areas: geographic information systems and civic data; evidence and research tooling; creative and practical applications.

How it is built. Small, testable pieces, written and verified by one developer. Claims about the software are meant to be reproducible, not persuasive.

Where it stands. Early. One working prototype runs locally, other work is in progress, and no product is generally available.

What we are building

Work, and the stage it is at

Updated 12 November 2026. Nothing on this page links to a product you cannot actually open.

Working · local prototype

Civic GIS prototype

An offline-first mapping application: layers and legend, an attribute table, spatial queries over local features, feature editing with undo, GeoJSON import and export, field observation capture, and a read-only connector tested against public ArcGIS REST services.

It runs on a local machine. It is not hosted, has no accounts, and holds no municipal data. Its automated test suite passes, and it was built to understand what a municipal GIS workflow actually requires.

In development

Evidence and research tooling

Software for assembling sources, keeping corrections and revisions attached to what they change, and making the difference between a claim and a finding visible.

This work is not open to new users. Earlier experiments in this area are closed while the foundations are rebuilt; no sign-up is offered here.

Various stages

Creative and practical applications

Smaller tools and experiments — some finished and used privately, some prototypes, some sketches that may never ship.

They are listed here as a category rather than as products, because none of them is ready to be presented as one.
