# Voalt: Energize Your Intelligence

**Brand direction (owner-selected):** **“Voalt: Energize Your Intelligence”**

Voalt is a provenance-centered, human-and-automation-assisted knowledge network. It should be useful even before anyone contributes, with cited subject feeds, public records, corrections and independent topical projects.

## Three interoperable apps

- **Voalt Atlas** — a standalone, local-first collaborative map/overlay tool that can link reviewed geographic evidence to Voalt subjects, entities and canonical items. A standalone map must still function without Voalt.
- **Voalt E-Reader** — a free, ad-free, offline-first reading app and a reusable web-reading interface for Voalt. Local documents remain private unless a user explicitly uploads/publishes them.
- **Voalt Browser** — a privacy-first browser product with an **optional**, user-opened Voalt discussion/evidence layer. It must function normally without a Voalt account or Voalt service.

These are **product directions**, not assertions that the applications are already installed, released or securely deployed.

Canonical Voalt source IDs, subject IDs, review/rights states and corrections are shared only when users explicitly connect or publish. Standalone functions do not depend on an operational Voalt server.

**Repository approach:** Voalt and its private source remain separate. Reader and Browser reuse the existing private Open-E-Reader and Secure-Browser repositories (renaming can come later); Atlas gets a new dedicated private repository. This working branch does not configure DNS or publish code.
