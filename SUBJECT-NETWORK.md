# Voalt subject network — proposed first five surfaces

> **Development preview only.** The canonical Voalt platform is not publicly deployed, and the hostnames shown below are proposals, not working DNS.

Voalt's immediate release objective is a **useful read-only subject directory**. Accounts and human voting are not prerequisites for browsing, learning and subscribing to sourced information.

| Proposed surface | Purpose | Source/publisher relationship | First milestone |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photography Intelligence | Camera, lens, firmware, imaging research and industry updates | Voalt-owned subject view; source rights reviewed independently | Searchable metadata and attributed subject RSS |
| Neuroscience & Cognitive Science | Research papers, labs, preprints and cognitive science updates | Voalt-owned subject view; DOI/PMID and publication status | Distinguish journal results and preprints |
| Whale Intelligence Network | Individual whale and conservation research registry | Collaborator-operated publication; Voalt displays only approved/safe records | Public-safe metadata without sensitive coordinates |
| Power Line News | Public-interest news and government records | Independent publisher; opt-in structured syndication | Keep Watchlist, News and Government Records independent |
| Coronado Happenings | Local community events and sourced hyperlocal reporting | Independent publisher; opt-in structured syndication | Stable, attributed event/story metadata |

**Shared identity:** one canonical Voalt item can appear in more than one subverse without becoming multiple independent pieces of evidence. Each item retains its publisher, source, review/rights state, revision and correction/withdrawal history.

**Separate brands:** each site can maintain independent editorial rights and its existing domain. A proposed `*.voalt.com` view is a brand/route alias, never a different canonical data model.

**Delivery targets (proposed, not promises):**
1. V0: local read-only directory, five subject pages and canonical item detail.
2. V1: five controlled fixture publishers through a single versioned publication contract.
3. V2: RSS and read-only sourced metadata feeds, with rights-gated content.
4. V3: local site-skin previews using one reviewed manifest.

No public source or photograph is copied into this directory. No legal, scientific, commercial or clinical status is implied by a category heading. Local synthetic pilot examples are visibly labeled as fixtures.

**Implementation status:** this branch is public documentation only. The working integration pilot and contracts are retained in the separately controlled private repository; production deployment remains a separate decision.
