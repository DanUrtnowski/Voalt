# Voalt — public milestone record

Milestones listed here are publicly verifiable. This is not the development
changelog. Nothing is listed until it is genuinely reached.

## 2026-11-03 — Network scope and architecture

- Voalt's purpose restated and widened: a community publishing, knowledge,
  intelligence and automation-partner network, not a single-subject platform.
- Two space types defined and kept distinct: native Voalt communities, and
  independent partner websites integrating through an authenticated interface.
- Community identity separated from URL routing, domain ownership and hosting.
  **No domains, subdomains or wildcard DNS were provisioned.**
- Identity model defined for human users, automated collectors, publication
  agents, research assistants and project-owned organisational accounts.
- Provenance rules recorded, including canonical origin identifiers, idempotent
  imports, and the rule that one story syndicated to five sites is not five
  corroborations.
- Automated accounts explicitly excluded from casting ordinary human votes or
  manufacturing apparent consensus.
- Automation-partner boundaries recorded: opt-in, bounded, auditable
  capability grants with human review for consequential actions.
- Existing transparent-voting design preserved, including pseudonymous vote
  accountability and the 24-hour disclosure policy, both pending privacy review.
- Ten architecture documents completed in the private repository.
- A local/staging-only integration pilot implemented and tested: a partner
  publication delivers a synthetic item into a Voalt community, with duplicate
  suppression, correction and withdrawal propagation, and publisher-side
  resilience when the network is unavailable.
- Private development repository initialised with architecture and pilot.

### Not yet started

Public deployment, accounts, live cross-posting, capability exchange between
